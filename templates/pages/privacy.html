Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

At our core, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy outlines how we handle your information transparently, securely, and in compliance with global data protection standards.

1. Information We Collect & How We Use It

We only collect customer data that is strictly necessary to fulfill your requests and optimize your platform experience. This includes:

  • Order Processing: Utilizing your delivery address, contact information, and billing details to securely execute purchases.
  • Service Optimization: Analyzing behavioral telemetry to improve interface speed, product recommendations, and customer support responsiveness.

2. Third-Party Data Protection

Your trust is paramount. We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. Data is only shared with trusted logistical partners (e.g., courier services) explicitly required to deliver your physical orders.

3. Security Infrastructure

All transmitted data is encrypted using industry-standard Secure Socket Layer (SSL/TLS) technology. We maintain strict digital guardrails to protect your stored records against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

4. Your Rights & Control

You retain full ownership of your data. You may request access to, correction of, or permanent deletion of your personal records at any time by contacting our data protection officer via our official support channels.
