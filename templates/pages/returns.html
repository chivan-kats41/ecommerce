Returns & Refunds Policy

We want you to love your purchase. If something isn't right, here is how we can fix it.

Standard Return Window: Eligible items can be returned within 7 calendar days from the date of delivery.

The Return Process

1

Verify Item Eligibility

Ensure products are completely unused, unaltered, and retained in their intact original brand packaging with all tags attached.

2

Request Support Approval

Contact our central support desk to log your request. Once verified, you will be issued an official Return Merchandise Authorization code.

3

Dispatch & Inspection

Ship the approved item back to our fulfillment center. Following a brief quality assurance assessment, your refund or exchange will be executed.

Condition Matrix

Item Condition Return Status
Unopened + Original Box Full Refund / Exchange
Opened but Unused Subject to Inspection
Used, Damaged, or Missing Tags Not Eligible
